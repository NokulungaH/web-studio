FAQ

Questions before we build?

How long does an ecommerce website take?

Timelines depend on catalogue size, content, integrations and feedback. We confirm the expected delivery plan before the build starts.

Will I be able to manage my own products and orders?

Yes. Ecommerce projects are designed so the business can manage products, stock and orders after handover, with ongoing care available if preferred.

Do you set up payments and delivery?

Yes. Payment and delivery requirements are scoped with the store and tested before launch. Merchant accounts remain associated with the client's business.

What happens after launch?

We can provide ongoing website care covering agreed maintenance, updates, backups and support.
