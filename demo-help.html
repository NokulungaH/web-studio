Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Our demo standard delivery is 3–5 working days, with an express option of 1–2 working days.

How can I track my order?

In a real store, customers receive tracking details by email once the order has been dispatched.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact the store as soon as possible. Orders that have already been dispatched may not be changeable.

What payment methods do you accept?

This demo simulates card, Yoco and PayFast payment options. No real payment is processed.

Do I need an account to order?

A real store can be configured for guest checkout, customer accounts, or both.

Returns & refunds

For this fictional demonstration, eligible unused items may be returned within 7 days of delivery. A real client's return and refund terms would be configured according to the products sold and applicable requirements.

  • Items should be unused and in original condition.
  • Some product categories may have exclusions where appropriate.
  • Approved refunds would be returned using the applicable payment process.

Shipping & delivery

The demo checkout offers standard nationwide delivery, express delivery and collection. A production store can use flat rates, location-based rates, free-shipping thresholds and supported courier integrations.

Privacy

The Nala & Co demo does not submit or store checkout information. A production ecommerce site would need an appropriate privacy notice explaining what personal information is collected, why it is used, who it may be shared with and how customers can exercise applicable rights.
